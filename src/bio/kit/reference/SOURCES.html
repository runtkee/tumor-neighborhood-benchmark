The reference holds per-patient summary statistics derived by this kit from public datasets. It contains no cell-level data. Each source:

  • IMMUcan_IMC_panel1: IMMUcan consortium, imaging mass cytometry panel 1, 179 patients (breast, GI, GU, head & neck, thoracic). Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.12912567. License CC BY 4.0. Neutrophils = CD15+MPO+.
  • CODEX_CRC_Schurch2020: Schürch et al., Cell 2020, 35 colorectal cancer patients, CODEX. Mendeley Data 10.17632/mpjzbtfgfr.1. License CC BY 4.0. "Granulocytes" = CD15+ (includes eosinophils).
  • SPATCH_*: SPATCH platform benchmark, Nat Commun 2025 (doi 10.1038/s41467-025-64292-3), one patient per cancer (colon, ovarian, liver), Xenium 5K and CosMx 6K. Data portal spatch.pku-genomics.org. Data license not stated on the portal; only derived summary statistics are included here.
  • MARTEAU_CRC_IMC_core / _margin: Marteau et al., Cancer Cell 2025 (doi 10.1016/j.ccell.2025.12.003), colorectal cancer imaging mass cytometry, 37 patient IDs, 1000x1000 µm intratumoral and margin regions. BioImage Archive S-BIAD2208. License CC BY 4.0. Neutrophils = the authors' CD15-based label; marker values are the authors' 0-1 normalised intensities.
  • CERVILLA_*_xenium5k: Cervilla et al. 2025, six-cancer spatial platform comparison, Xenium Prime 5K sections (colorectal, lung, breast, ovarian; one section each), cell types called by this kit from marker genes. Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18000256. License CC BY 4.0.
  • MARTEAU_XENIUM_core / _margin / _normal: Marteau et al., Cancer Cell 2025, colorectal cancer Xenium (380-gene Immuno-Oncology panel), 15 patients, sections of tumor core, invasive margin and adjacent normal tissue; cell types called by this kit at cluster level from the 10x graph clusters. BioImage Archive S-BIAD2208. License CC BY 4.0. Neutrophils rest on CXCR1/CXCR2/S100A9/G0S2 (no CSF3R or FCGR3B in the panel).

Changes: all sources were re-processed with this kit's locked recipe (v3p.1). Cell-type labels were mapped to kit roles as listed in each dataset's manifest (kit_manifests/).

    Tumor Neighborhood Benchmark

    Do T cells keep away from neutrophils, gather near macrophages, or change state when touching them? Each dataset is measured the same way, one value per patient, and compared side by side.

    Runs in your browser. Loaded files are never uploaded.

    Method: 10 µm = touching, 30 µm = nearby. Every proximity test compares against cell labels shuffled within each image, so tissue layout is held fixed. Marker levels are within-image percentiles. A direction counts as consistent when at least 70% of patients (and at least 5) agree. Reference sources: IMMUcan (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.12912567, CC BY 4.0), Schürch et al. 2020 CODEX (Mendeley 10.17632/mpjzbtfgfr.1, CC BY 4.0), SPATCH benchmark (Nat Commun 2025, summary statistics only). Research use only; not for diagnosis.
